Who We Are

Crafting Heirlooms Since 2004

✦ ✦

Rinky Jewellers was founded in 2004 in the heart of Awas Vikas Colony, Kanpur, with a singular vision — to bring timeless elegance to every household in the city.

What started as a humble family workshop has grown into one of Kanpur's most trusted names in fine jewellery, built on three pillars: exceptional craftsmanship, pure quality, and unwavering trust.

For over two decades, our master artisans have handcrafted each piece with meticulous care — from grand bridal sets to delicate everyday accessories — ensuring that every item that leaves our workshop is a work of art.

Today, Rinky Jewellers is proudly carried forward by the second generation, preserving our cherished family legacy while bringing fresh ideas, contemporary aesthetics, and modern developments to the business.
